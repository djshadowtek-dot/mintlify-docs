Data innovation, as embodied by The Awesome, means giving every team — not just data scientists — the ability to extract meaningful insights from their data without manual coding or complex tooling. The platform automates the repetitive, technical work so you can focus on making decisions.

## The Data Innovation Lifecycle

Move from raw data to actionable insight through four repeatable stages.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Link your data sources — databases, files, APIs, or cloud storage — to The Awesome. Each connection is saved and reusable across any number of analyses and workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrich">
    The platform automatically normalizes and prepares your data. Type inference, deduplication, and schema alignment happen without any manual intervention on your part.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze">
    AI models surface the patterns that matter: trends, anomalies, correlations, and forecasts. You choose the model types; The Awesome handles the computation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act">
    Export your results, trigger downstream webhooks, or build custom applications on top of the API. Insights flow to wherever your team already works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Sources

A data source is a saved connection to your raw data. Once you create a connection, The Awesome can read from it on demand or on a schedule — no repeated uploads or credential re-entry required.

The following source types are supported:

| Category | Supported Options |
|---|---|
| **File Uploads** | CSV, Excel (`.xlsx`), JSON |
| **Databases** | PostgreSQL, MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server, BigQuery |
| **APIs** | Any REST API that returns JSON responses |
| **Cloud Storage** | Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage |

Connect a new source from **Data Sources → Add Source** in the sidebar. The Awesome guides you through credentials, connection testing, and optional field mapping in a single setup flow.

## Data Privacy & Security

Your data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Every analysis job runs inside an isolated compute environment — your data is never co-mingled with another customer's workload.

You retain full ownership of all data you bring into The Awesome. The platform never uses your data to train shared or third-party models. Your data is used solely to produce the results you request.

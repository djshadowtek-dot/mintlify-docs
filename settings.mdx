The Settings panel in The Awesome gives you control over every aspect of your workspace — from display preferences and data retention to security policies and billing. Navigate to **Settings** from the left sidebar to access all configuration options.

## General Settings

Customize the following options to match your team's preferences:

- **Workspace Name**: The display name for your organization's workspace.
- **Default Timezone**: Sets the timezone used for scheduled workflows and timestamp display.
- **Language**: Interface language preference (English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese).
- **Date Format**: Choose between `YYYY-MM-DD`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, or `DD/MM/YYYY`.

## Data Retention

By default, The Awesome retains analysis results and raw source data for 90 days. You can adjust this window at any time to meet your team's storage and compliance requirements — or permanently remove all stored data if needed.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Adjust Retention Period">
    Navigate to **Settings → Data & Privacy → Retention Policy** and choose one of the available retention windows: 30, 60, 90, 180, or 365 days.

    Changes to your retention policy apply to **new data only**. Existing data retains its original time-to-live (TTL) and will expire according to the policy that was active when it was ingested.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Delete All Data">
    To immediately remove all stored data from your workspace, click **Purge All Data** from the **Settings → Data & Privacy → Retention Policy** page.

    <Warning>
      Purging data is permanent and cannot be undone. Export any results you need before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

Keep your workspace secure by enabling two-factor authentication and reviewing active sessions regularly.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Enable 2FA from **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**. The Awesome supports both authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS-based verification. Once enabled, every sign-in requires a one-time code in addition to your password.

<Tip>
  Use an authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy) rather than SMS for stronger security.
</Tip>

### Session Management

Review all active sessions for your account under **Settings → Security → Sessions**. Each entry shows the device, browser, and approximate location. To close a specific session, click **Revoke** next to it. To sign out of all other devices at once, click **Revoke All Other Sessions**.

## Billing & Plan

Manage your subscription from **Settings → Billing**. This page displays your current plan, key usage metrics (analyses run, storage used), and available upgrade options.

<Note>
  Plan upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades are applied at the end of the current billing period.
</Note>

## Research Assistant

Use GPT to summarize supplied papers, compare methods, and identify questions worth investigating. Ask it to distinguish source-supported findings from interpretations and untested hypotheses.

**Starter prompt:** “Compare these papers on retrieval-augmented generation. For each, list the research question, dataset, baseline, findings, limitations, and supporting passages. Flag missing information rather than guessing.”

## Evaluation Lab

Create a fixed evaluation set and a scoring rubric before comparing models. Separate automated checks from human judgments, and record failures as well as successful answers.

## Learning Tutor

Build lessons and exercises around your current research topic. Ask for explanations at different levels, then test understanding with worked problems and feedback.

**Starter prompt:** “Teach me how precision and recall apply to document retrieval. Start with a practical example, give me an exercise, and explain the tradeoffs after I answer.”

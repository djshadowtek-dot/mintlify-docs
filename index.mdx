## The three-move loop

Raw data in. Decisions out. Three moves, no ceremony.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect anything">
    PostgreSQL, MySQL, S3, REST APIs, CSVs — plug it in, we normalize it. One unified model, ready for the AI to attack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unleash the analysis">
    Pick a source, pick an analysis (Trend, Anomaly, Correlation), hit run. The engine hunts patterns while you keep working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act before it goes cold">
    Charts, plain-language explanations, downloadable reports, webhooks into your stack. Insight to action, same session.
  </Step>
</Steps>
